- Created a **repeatable ETL pattern for eligibility operations** that balances flexibility and governance, demonstrating strong data engineering judgment in a domain where partner heterogeneity is a core operational challenge.
- Improved pipeline robustness by standardizing cleaned output into a final production-ready eligibility table, making the data more usable for downstream business and analytics consumers.
- Showed ownership of both **pipeline design and operational usability** by structuring the project for scalability, partner onboarding, and data-quality transparency rather than one-off file processing.
